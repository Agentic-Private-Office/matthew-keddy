# Agentic Private Office — Matthew Keddy

The Agentic Private Office of Matthew Keddy holds a portfolio of AI-native brands in three markets: capital markets, consumer and retail AI shopping, and pharma. Together they generate 100M+ in AI agent and human traffic a month, across global markets in the Americas, the EU and UK, and Asia. That is the scale of the top finance and retail websites in North America, reached with a new generation of products.

IRL agent-ready data products: the record of real things, built for AI agents that act in real life. Not training data for the labs. Not synthetic data from a model. Real products, real issuers and real medicines, each with its source.

The record is served live on Model Context Protocol (MCP), in real time on Microsoft Azure across 20+ hyperscale cloud regions, with Amazon Web Services held as standby.

An agentic private office combines the people of the traditional office with the agentic assets of the new era — the agents, the keys, the tenancies and the record — under one signature.
